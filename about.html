Rameshji's story

Ramesh Sharma used to fix bikes at his father's repair shop near Kutchery Road. Every Urs season he saw tourists stuck at the station, haggling with autos, missing half of what Ajmer has to show.

In 2014 he bought two second-hand Activas, painted a board by hand and started renting them out. Ten years later the shop has 40+ bikes, but Rameshji still checks every brake himself before a bike leaves.

"Agar aap meri bike pe ghoomne gaye ho, to aap mere mehmaan ho."
