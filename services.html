Rental terms

Short and simple, please read once.

  • A valid original driving license is a must (two-wheeler category). We'll keep a photocopy.
  • One original ID (Aadhaar / passport / voter ID) is kept as security for zero-deposit rentals.
  • Helmets must be worn by rider and pillion. Two helmets given free.
  • Fuel policy: bike given with fuel, return it with the same level. Or we charge at pump rate.
  • Rental day = 24 hours from pickup. Late by more than 2 hours? Half day charged.
  • Riding outside Rajasthan needs prior permission.
